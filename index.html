About NetPulse

NetPulse is a free browser-based internet speed test. It measures download speed, upload speed, ping and jitter to help you understand how your connection is performing.

Download measures how quickly data reaches your device. Upload measures how quickly your device sends data. Ping measures response time, while jitter measures variation in response time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does download speed mean?

Download speed describes how quickly data can be received by your device. It is measured in Mbps.

What does upload speed mean?

Upload speed describes how quickly your device can send data to a server.

What is ping?

Ping is the response time between your device and the test server. Lower values generally mean a more responsive connection.

What is jitter?

Jitter describes how much the measured response time changes between samples.

Why can my result change?

Network traffic, Wi-Fi signal strength, device activity and server conditions can all affect a speed test.

Does NetPulse store my speed?

The current version displays your measurements in the browser and does not include a NetPulse account or results database.
