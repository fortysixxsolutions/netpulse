About NetPulse

NetPulse is a simple, free internet speed testing tool that helps you understand how your connection is performing. It measures download speed, upload speed, ping and jitter directly in your browser.

Download speed describes how quickly data reaches your device. Upload speed describes how quickly your device sends data. Ping measures response time, while jitter describes changes in response time.
